Welcome back

Yalla Falaina is for job and immigration seekers 18 and older. The concierge connects you with real opportunities — it never replaces a licensed professional.
Basic — $25/month. The concierge is a subscriber feature: real matched opportunities, logged answers, daily fair-use quota.

If you cancel, you keep access until the end of the paid period, then have 30 days to come back with everything as you left it. After that, your profile and intake details are permanently deleted — your account itself stays.
