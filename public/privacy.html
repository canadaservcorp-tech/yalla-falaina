1. What we collect

CategoryExamples
AccountEmail, name, phone number, hashed password (we never store your password itself)
Profile & intakePreferred language/country/sector, work history, education, certifications, languages spoken, and yes/no answers about passport, visa, legal residency, and family or host contacts abroad — collected to power matching, not to gatekeep

2. Why we collect it

  • To run the concierge and match you to real opportunities.
  • To keep a durable record of what the concierge told you — this protects you (a record you can point to) and it protects the accuracy of our service.
  • To bill your subscription and enforce fair-use limits.
  • To detect fraud, abuse, and prohibited listings.
  • To meet legal and accounting obligations.

3. Who we share it with

We don't sell your personal information. It's shared only with the service providers that make Yalla Nsafer work, each acting on our instructions:

  • Anthropic — powers the concierge; your messages are sent to their API to generate a reply.
  • PayPal — processes your subscription payment; we receive your subscription status, not your payment details.
  • Resend — sends account and transactional emails (verification, receipts, retention notices).
  • Supabase — hosts our database and file storage.
  • Licensed job-feed providers (e.g. Adzuna, Jooble) supply listings to us — we don't send your information to them.

Because these providers and our own infrastructure may process data outside your country of residence (including in Canada and the United States), your information may cross borders in the course of providing the service.

4. How long we keep it

While your account is active, we keep your profile and conversation history to keep the concierge useful. If your subscription lapses or you cancel, we keep your profile and intake data for 30 days — enough time to come back without starting over — after which we permanently delete it (your work history, documents, and conversation-derived answers). Your account and login are kept so you can always sign back in; if you'd like your account itself closed, contact us (below).

Uploaded documents (when that feature is enabled) carry their own, shorter retention window and are deleted automatically once it passes.

5. Your rights

You can ask us, at any time, to access, correct, or delete your personal information, or to tell you exactly what we hold about you. Quebec residents additionally have rights under Law 25, including the right to file a complaint with the Commission d'accès à l'information if you believe we've handled your information improperly. Contact us first — most requests can be resolved directly.

6. Security

Passwords are hashed (never stored in plain text), traffic to and from the site is encrypted, and our database is accessed only through server-side credentials — never exposed to the browser.

7. Children

Yalla Nsafer is for people 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone younger.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the service evolves; a material change will be reflected in a new version date at the top of this page.
